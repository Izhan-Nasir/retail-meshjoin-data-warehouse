## Q1: Top 5 Products by Revenue with Weekday/Weekend Monthly Drill-Down

### The Business Question
*"Who are our 5 biggest cash-cow products across the entire year, and when do customers buy them—during the working week or on weekends, month-by-month?"*

### Output Columns
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `product_id` | Unique catalog SKU code (e.g. `22423`). |
| `product_name` | Commercial item title. |
| `year`, `month` | Calendar period of the sales. |
| `day_type` | `Weekday` (Monday–Friday) vs. `Weekend` (Saturday–Sunday). |
| `monthly_revenue` | Total revenue (£) generated by that product in that specific month and day type. |

### Sample Output Explained
```
product_id   product_name                year  month day_type  monthly_revenue
22423        REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER    2010     12  Weekday         12540.15
22423        REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER    2010     12  Weekend          1707.60
22423        REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER    2011      1  Weekday          9312.45
22423        REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER    2011      1  Weekend           882.60
23166        MEDIUM CERAMIC STORAGE JAR  2011      1  Weekday         77183.60
```
- **What this tells us:** 
  1. The `REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER` generated **£12,540.15** during December 2010 weekdays, but only **£1,707.60** on weekends. This represents an ~88% weekday skew.
  2. In January 2011, `MEDIUM CERAMIC TOP STORAGE JAR` had a massive bulk corporate purchase of **£77,183.60** on weekdays.
- **Business Action:** High-value gift items (like cake stands) sell primarily on weekdays (often corporate gifting or online evening shopping before Friday delivery). Focus digital marketing spend on Monday through Thursday.

## Q17: Hierarchical Multi-Level Revenue Rollup

### The Business Question
*"Can we view product sales, supplier subtotals, store subtotals, and the overall company grand total all in one report?"*

### Output Columns
| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `store_name` | Individual store, or `--- ALL STORES (GRAND TOTAL) ---`. |
| `supplier_name` | Individual supplier, or `--- ALL SUPPLIERS (STORE TOTAL) ---`. |
| `product_name` | Individual product, or `--- ALL PRODUCTS (SUPPLIER TOTAL) ---`. |
| `total_revenue` | Sum of revenue at that aggregation tier. |

### How It Works:
Uses SQL `GROUP BY store_name, supplier_name, product_name WITH ROLLUP`. `COALESCE()` labels `NULL` summary rows into human-readable headers:
1. **Tier 1:** Individual product total within supplier and store.
2. **Tier 2:** Supplier subtotal across all its products in that store.
3. **Tier 3:** Store subtotal across all suppliers.
4. **Tier 4:** Grand Total across all stores in the enterprise.
