3. MASTER DATA GENERATION (DETERMINISTIC HASHING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Master data attributes were generated deterministically using MD5 hashes via stable():
  stable(key, n) = int(MD5(utf-8(key))[:8], 16) % n

Customer Master Data (customer_master_data.csv):
  - Total Unique Customers: 4,338
  - Partition Size: 500 tuples
  - Number of Partitions: ceil(4,338 / 500) = 9 partitions
  - Generated Attributes: gender, age_group, occupation, city_category,
    stay_in_current_city_years, marital_status.

Product Master Data (product_master_data.csv):
  - Total Unique Products: 3,665
  - Partition Size: 500 tuples
  - Number of Partitions: ceil(3,665 / 500) = 8 partitions
  - Generated Attributes: product_name (longest description), product_category,
    supplier_id, supplier_name.


4. STREAM PARTITIONING & SALES MODE SPLIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sales mode was assigned deterministically at the cart (invoice) level:
  hash = int(MD5(str(int(invoice_no)))[:8], 16) % 10
  0 - 6 -> Online   (70.1% of transactions)
  7 - 9 -> Periodic (29.9% of transactions)

Distribution:
  - Online Transactions (online_sales_stream.csv): 275,154 rows
  - Periodic Transactions (periodic_sales_data table): 117,538 rows
  - Periodic Groups: 48 groups staged across release_hour 0..47 (~2,449 rows/group)
  - Total Transactions: 392,692 rows
